# Editorial photography

Six photographs are downloaded locally and linked to their creators in the page. The first four were selected from pages explicitly marked Free to use under the Unsplash License. Two photographic interpretations of original looks were selected from Pexels and visually reviewed before use.

- **Dion Martins**: [original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/man-in-stylish-outfit-walks-down-street-X-Vp-swqmKQ) - Featured hero and Expressive tailoring gallery look. Local asset: `assets/editorial/expressive-layers.jpg`.
- **Karla Martinez**: [original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/two-people-walking-in-the-street-CCRw_jYeUoY) - Personal-style journal story. Local asset: `assets/editorial/street-style-duo.jpg`.
- **Long Chung**: [original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/man-wearing-a-suede-jacket-white-shirt-and-jeans-Gc5Rpu9186o) - Texture journal story. Local asset: `assets/editorial/suede-and-denim.jpg`.
- **Max Omen**: [original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-woman-walks-towards-the-camera-outdoors-V7oBvq1qsxw) - City ease gallery look and casual-style journal story. Local asset: `assets/editorial/city-streetwear.jpg`.

- **Kristina Chuprina**: [original photograph](https://www.pexels.com/photo/female-model-wearing-jeans-and-plastic-black-jacket-posing-in-street-10219553/) - Denim days photographic interpretation. Local asset: `assets/editorial/denim-days.jpg`.
- **Hamit Ferhat**: [original photograph](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-woman-in-white-long-sleeves-and-black-pants-standing-on-the-roadside-while-looking-at-the-camera-14620818/) - Back to basics photographic interpretation. Local asset: `assets/editorial/back-to-basics.jpg`.
